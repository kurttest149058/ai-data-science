With this project, I want to try and figure out which areas of San Francisco are the most vulnerable to floods and find ways to help them
prepare for it. This topic is very interesting to me because we just saw the floods in Nepal that caused so much damage, so we should try
to prevent that much damage from happening in the future. Nature is very unpredictable so even a little bit of foresight could help a lot.
The data that I will need is about which groups/demographics of people are in dangerous zones so I can figure out the best things to do
for them based on their needs and limits. I expect to use data that includes the percentages of different categories for each region.
