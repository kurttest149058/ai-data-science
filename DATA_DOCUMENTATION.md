Source: DataSF open portal, https://www.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/FloodVulnerabilityReport_v5.pdf.pdf
Size: 579 rows, 27 columns
Column meanings:  
Census Blockgroup: the indicating number for that specific group of people that live in an area
Children: percent of people that are children
Children_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
Elderly: percent of people 65+
Elderly_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
Nonwhite: percent of people that are not white
Nonwhite_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
Poverty: percent of people living below 200% of the federal poverty line
Poverty_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
Education: percent of people 25+ with at least high school education
Education_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
English: percent of people who have limited english speaking abilities
English_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
Elevation: lowest elevation in that area
SeaLevelRise: percent of land that is within 36 inches of the sea level rise
Precipitation: percent of land that might get over 6 inches of rain in a 100 year storm
Diabetes: rate of adults 18+ that are hospitalized due to diabetes
Mental Health: hospitalization rate due to mental health diseases/disorders
Asthma: hospitalization rate due to asthma
Disability: percent of people with disabilities
Disability_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
HousingQuality: yearly housing violations per 1000 residents
Homeless: number of homeless people per 1000 residents
LivAlone: percent of people who live alone
LivAlone_wNULLvalues: if blank then the source for that category cannot be backed up
FloodHealthIndex: summary of overall flood vulnerability (high number = more vulnerable)
Note: combines flood exposure, socioeconomic/demographic vulnerability, health vulnerability, and housing vulnerability into a standardized indicator that measures relativity to other blockgroups
FloodHealthIndex_Quintiles: puts the vulnerability on a scale of 1-5 with 5 being the highest
